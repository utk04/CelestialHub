Who Are We?

At Celestial Hub, we are innovators, dreamers, and educators reimagining how the universe is experienced. Our state-of-the-art 3D VFX hall uses holographic projections and VR to transport audiences into the cosmos—offering a surreal, real-time journey through galaxies, planets, and cosmic phenomena. Combining entertainment, science, and technology, we aim to inspire curiosity and make space accessible to all.
